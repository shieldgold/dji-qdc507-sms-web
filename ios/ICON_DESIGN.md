# 小电波 · App 名称与图标

0.3.1 起显示名称由“模块电话”改为“小电波”。英文工程名与 Bundle ID 保持原值，覆盖更新原 App，不新建另一个 App。

## 视觉

- 亮橙色满版背景，黑色电波小怪兽、米白色眼睛、橙色负形笑脸。
- 大块、连续的三波峰轮廓，减少小细节，方便在桌面的小尺寸图标中找到。
- App 图标：`ModulePhone/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`，1024 × 1024、RGB、不透明；iOS 自动裁切外部圆角。
- 首页标识：`ModulePhone/Assets.xcassets/ModuleMark.imageset/ModuleMark.png`，120 × 120。
- App 内强调色改为深橙色，以保留白色通话按钮图形的对比度。
- 原图标保留在 Git 历史；新资源由内置图像生成工具生成，再按资产尺寸缩放。

## 生成提示

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished premium iOS app icon for a personal cellular calling and SMS app named 小电波 (Little Radio Wave). The old green phone-and-SIM icon was rejected for being ugly and hard to spot; create a completely NEW, distinct identity. Visual: saturated electric tangerine orange full-bleed background (#FF641C), with a bold oversized ink-black friendly radio-wave creature in the center. Invent a charismatic simple mascot silhouette shaped like a flowing chunky three-lobed sound wave or squiggly blob, slightly asymmetric and energetic, with two very clear ivory oval eyes and a cheeky small smile formed as negative space. One compact strong silhouette, strong memorable gesture, designer toy personality but elegant contemporary graphic branding. The black creature fills about 65% of the canvas, with generous consistent breathing room. Extremely legible at 48px among other home-screen icons. Flat clean vector-like edges with a very restrained tactile finish; no busy detail, no elaborate shading, no gradients that muddy the orange. No telephone handset, no SIM card drawing, no wireless/radio arc symbols, no typography, no letters, no words, no logos of existing brands. Exact square 1024x1024 opaque artwork. The orange background must cover all four corners: NO rounded outer app-icon mask, NO border, NO outer white margins, NO mockup, NO phone, NO grid. Deliver the icon artwork alone, full canvas.
