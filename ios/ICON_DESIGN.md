## 最终生成提示

Use case: logo-brand. Create the finished iOS app icon for a personal app called 模块电话, which uses a small cellular module for phone calls and SMS. A single distinctive, beautifully simple white telephone handset merged with a rounded rectangular SIM/module silhouette and one small signal accent, readable at 40 pixels. Premium calm industrial design. Saturated deep teal to emerald background with extremely subtle dimensional lighting, crisp smooth white symbol centered and generously spaced. Exact square canvas, full bleed opaque background, no external rounded-corner mask (iOS will apply it), no border, no letters, no text, no watermark, no app store mockup, no surrounding phone. One finished 1024x1024 icon only, not a presentation or grid. Keep the central mark compact and bold, avoid thin lines or tiny detail.
